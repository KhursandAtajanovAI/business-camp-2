# Roadmap: from one birth to the family's next years

Today the product checks the papers of one birth. The plan is to stay useful to the same
family for two to three years. Everything below the first row is a plan, not a product.
Prices, dates and retention numbers are **our assumptions**, not data.

## The family path

| Family event | What the site does | When |
|---|---|---|
| The birth: hospital and doctor bills | Check, claim letter, call script, case file | in the product now |
| Insurance explanations (EOBs) weeks later | Compare with the bill, remind about replies | in the product now; appeal deadlines next |
| Adding the baby to the insurance plan | A reminder about the enrollment window. The length of the window must be checked against an official source before it is shown as a fact | later |
| Well-baby visits and vaccines | Check preventive-care bills, with a sourced rule | later |
| Open enrollment and FSA/HSA dates | Reminders about dates the family sets | later |
| The next child | A free check before the birth, then a new case | in two to three years |

Any rule about insurance or deadlines is first checked against an official source.
Until then it is marked "worth asking", never "an error on your bill".

## Plans (assumptions)

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Start | $29 once, or $14.99 a month while the case is open | the birth case (in the product now) |
| Family | $6.99 a month or $59 a year | after the case closes: first-year bills and reminders |
| Pause | free for up to 6 months | instead of cancelling |

Illustration, not a forecast: a family pays $33.79 for the first case; if 30% stay through
months 4 to 12 and 12% are still there at month 36, that is about $73 over three years.

## Reasons to come back

A pause instead of a cancellation, a free month for returning, a yearly summary of what was
checked, a free check before the next child, early access to new rules.
Referrals: "give a month, get a month", credited after the friend's first payment, up to 12 months.

## What we will measure

Retention at months 3, 12 and 36, the share of pauses, the share of families who invite
someone, and how many invited families pay.

## Not planned

Other kinds of bills outside birth and the first years, sign-up walls, medical or legal advice,
any promise of a refund. B2B (employers, doulas) only as a channel; the parent stays the payer.

The types for this path already exist in `src/contracts/family.ts`.
