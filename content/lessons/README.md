# content/lessons

Owner: Dias.

Words for the calm corners of the site, in English.

- `lessons.ts` — micro-lessons, about one minute of reading each. `{{places}}` are filled from the person's own papers.
- `game.ts` — the script of the detective game: a made-up bill, its four clues, hints and words.
- `lessons.test.ts`, `game.test.ts` — checks the length, the own-example rule and the calm wording.

Import `lessons` from `lessons.ts` and `gameBill`, `gameWords` from `game.ts`.
