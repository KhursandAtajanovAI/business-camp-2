# content/letters

Owner: Khursand. The words of letters and call scripts, in one place, in English.
Code in `src/letters` fills the `{{places}}` and never writes user-facing text itself.

- `disclaimer.ts` — the one approved disclaimer.
- `claim-letter.ts` — the letter that asks a billing office to review charges.
- `call-script.ts` — what to say, what to write down and what to ask on the phone.

Rules for every text here: calm, polite, "amount in question" instead of a promise
of money back, no medical or legal advice.
